NexDare Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how NexDare handles information when you use the NexDare website or app. NexDare is currently presented under the NexDare name. For privacy questions or requests, contact nexdare.support@gmail.com.

Important: This policy describes data flows visible in the current app. Some service-provider retention periods and processing locations are controlled by those providers and may change.

1. Information the app may handle

  • Account information: email address, authentication identifiers and account status used to create and secure your account.
  • Profile information: username, display name, bio, avatar, gender selection and profile photos that you choose to provide.
  • Social and app activity: friend requests, friend lists, blocks, group membership, presence/last-seen settings, stories, story views, game rooms, scores and related app data.
  • Communications: messages and attachments you send through chats. The app uses encryption for supported direct and group chat content; not every item of account, profile, presence or game metadata is end-to-end encrypted.
  • Device/browser storage: preferences and app state such as language, theme, sound and notification settings. Authentication and encryption-key storage behavior depends on the platform and browser capabilities.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and authenticate accounts, display profiles, connect friends, operate chats and groups, deliver stories and games, maintain presence features, remember preferences, protect the service and respond to support requests.

3. Service providers and sharing

NexDare uses third-party services to operate app features. These include Google Firebase services for authentication and database hosting, EmailJS for sending account verification emails, Google Translate for optional interface translation, and Jitsi Meet when a user starts an audio/video meeting. Those providers may process information necessary for the feature you use under their own privacy terms. The app does not need to send chat message text to Google Translate for normal chat use.

Information may also be visible to people you choose to interact with, such as friends, group members or people who can view a story based on your in-app settings. Do not share information you do not want other users to see.

4. Encryption and security

The app implements encryption for supported chat content. Encryption does not mean that all data in the app is encrypted end-to-end: account identifiers, profile information, friend/group relationships, presence, and some operational metadata are processed separately. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account credentials and device secure, and report suspected account misuse to the support email above.

5. Retention and account deletion

Information is generally kept while it is needed to provide the service or meet legitimate operational and legal requirements. The app provides an account-deletion flow that may include a 15-day pending period before scheduled deletion. Deletion may not immediately remove information that is part of another user's records, shared conversations, provider backups, or records a provider must retain. Where complete removal requires action by a service provider, processing may take additional time.

6. Your choices

  • You can update profile details and privacy-related settings in the app.
  • You can manage notification permission in your device or browser settings.
  • You can request help with account access, correction or deletion by emailing nexdare.support@gmail.com.

7. Children's privacy

NexDare is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without the consent required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

8. International processing

Because NexDare relies on third-party infrastructure, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. The safeguards and terms of those providers may apply.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this address with an updated date.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: nexdare.support@gmail.com.
