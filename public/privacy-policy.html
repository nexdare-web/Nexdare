NexDare Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how NexDare handles information when you use the NexDare website or app. NexDare is currently presented under the NexDare name. For privacy questions or requests, contact nexdare.support@gmail.com.

Important: This policy describes data flows visible in the current app. Some service-provider retention periods and processing locations are controlled by those providers and may change.

1. Information the app may handle

  • Account information: email address, authentication identifiers and account status used to create and secure your account.
  • Profile information: username, display name, bio, avatar, gender selection and profile photos that you choose to provide.
  • Social and app activity: friend requests, friend lists, blocks, group membership, presence/last-seen settings, stories, story views, game rooms, scores and related app data.
  • Communications: messages and attachments you send through chats. The app uses encryption for supported direct and group chat content; not every item of account, profile, presence or game metadata is end-to-end encrypted.
  • Device/browser storage: preferences such as language, theme, sound and notification settings, plus temporary app cache. Depending on browser support, authentication tokens and encryption-key material may be stored in IndexedDB; personal cached profile/app data is kept in session storage where available. If a browser does not support a storage feature, behavior may differ.
  • Camera and microphone: the app can access your camera or microphone only when you choose a feature that needs it and grant the browser/device permission. Audio/video meetings open through Jitsi Meet.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and authenticate accounts, display profiles, connect friends, operate chats and groups, deliver stories and games, maintain presence features, remember preferences, protect the service and respond to support requests.

3. Service providers and sharing

NexDare uses Google Firebase services for account authentication and database hosting. Firebase Authentication sends account-verification and password-reset emails. The app code also contains an optional EmailJS integration for account-deletion OTP delivery, but its service, template and public-key settings are currently blank in this release; that OTP option is therefore not active unless the integration is configured in a future release. Google Translate may process text you explicitly submit for optional interface translation. Jitsi Meet is opened when you choose to start an audio/video meeting. These providers process information needed for the feature you use under their own terms and privacy policies. Normal chat message text is not sent to Google Translate as part of ordinary chat use.

Information may also be visible to people you choose to interact with, such as friends, group members or people who can view a story based on your in-app settings. Do not share information you do not want other users to see.

4. Encryption and security

The app implements encryption for supported direct and group chat content. Encryption does not mean that all data in the app is encrypted end-to-end: account identifiers, profile information, friend/group relationships, presence, game activity and some operational metadata are processed separately. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account credentials and device secure, and report suspected account misuse to the support email above.

5. Retention and account deletion

Information is generally kept while it is needed to provide the service or meet legitimate operational and legal requirements. When you request permanent account deletion in the current app, the account is marked as pending deletion and deactivated for a 15-day waiting period. Signing in before that period ends cancels the pending request. In this release, final deletion is processed when the account next attempts to sign in after the 15-day period; there is no separate background deletion scheduler. If you do not sign in again after the waiting period, or if deletion processing reports an error, contact nexdare.support@gmail.com so the request can be reviewed. Deletion removes account-owned records the app is able to remove, but may not immediately remove information in another user's records, shared conversations, provider backups, or records a provider must retain. Provider-side deletion and backups may take additional time.

6. Your choices

  • You can update profile details and privacy-related settings in the app.
  • You can manage notification permission in your device or browser settings.
  • You can request help with account access, correction or deletion by emailing nexdare.support@gmail.com.

7. Children's privacy

NexDare is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without the consent required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

8. International processing

Because NexDare relies on third-party infrastructure, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. The safeguards and terms of those providers may apply.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this address with an updated date.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: nexdare.support@gmail.com.
